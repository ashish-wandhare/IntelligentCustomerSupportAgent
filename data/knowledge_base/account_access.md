# Account Access and Login Support

## 1. Password Reset

Problem: A customer cannot sign in because they forgot their password.

Recommended steps:
1. Open the organization's official sign-in page.
2. Select "Forgot Password".
3. Enter the registered email address.
4. Follow the password-reset instructions sent to that email.
5. Create a new, strong password.

Escalation: If the reset email does not arrive, check the spam folder and verify the registered email address. If the issue continues, escalate to the account support team.

## 2. Account Locked

Problem: A customer is locked out after several unsuccessful login attempts.

Recommended steps:
1. Stop repeated login attempts.
2. Follow the official account recovery process.
3. Complete any required identity verification.
4. If the account remains locked, contact the account support team.

Security: Never ask customers to share their passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), or authentication codes.

## 3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Problem: A customer cannot complete multi-factor authentication.

Recommended steps:
1. Verify that the device's date and time are correct.
2. Check whether the authenticator application is functioning.
3. If using an SMS code, confirm that the registered phone can receive messages.
4. Use the organization's approved MFA recovery procedure if access remains unavailable.

Escalation: Refer persistent MFA failures to the account security team. Identity verification is required before changing authentication settings.

## 4. Account Access Security

Support personnel must follow the organization's identity-verification policy before making account changes. Never disable security controls or bypass identity verification merely to resolve a ticket quickly.
