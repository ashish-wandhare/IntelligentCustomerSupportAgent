# Billing and Payment Support

## 1. Payment Failed

Problem: A customer attempted to make a payment, but the transaction failed.

Recommended steps:
1. Check the payment status in the approved billing system.
2. Confirm whether the payment was declined, failed, or is still pending.
3. Ask the customer to verify their payment method with their financial institution if necessary.
4. If the payment remains unresolved, escalate to the billing support team.

Important: Never ask customers to share their complete card number, PIN, CVV, banking password, or OTP.

## 2. Duplicate Payment

Problem: A customer believes they were charged more than once for the same transaction.

Recommended steps:
1. Collect the transaction references and approximate transaction dates.
2. Verify the transactions using the approved billing system.
3. Determine whether the entries represent completed charges or temporary authorizations.
4. If a duplicate charge is confirmed, follow the organization's refund or payment-correction policy.

Escalation: Refer unresolved or confirmed duplicate charges to the billing team.

## 3. Refund Status

Problem: A customer wants to know the status of a refund.

Recommended steps:
1. Locate the original transaction using approved verification procedures.
2. Check whether the refund was requested, approved, processed, or rejected.
3. Communicate the verified status and any published processing timeframe.
4. If the refund exceeds the expected timeframe, escalate to the billing team.

Important: Never promise a refund or a specific completion date unless the billing system or policy confirms it.

## 4. Incorrect Invoice

Problem: A customer reports an incorrect amount or an unexpected invoice.

Recommended steps:
1. Verify the invoice number and billing period.
2. Review the invoice line items and applicable account plan.
3. Compare the invoice with the organization's billing records.
4. Escalate discrepancies that cannot be resolved under the approved billing policy.

## 5. Billing Security and Privacy

Access billing records only through authorized systems and after following required identity-verification procedures. Do not expose another customer's payment information. Follow the organization's data protection and refund policies.
