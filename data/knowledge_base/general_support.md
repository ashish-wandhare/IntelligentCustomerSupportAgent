# General Customer Support

## 1. Update Customer Profile

Problem: A customer wants to update their profile information.

Recommended steps:
1. Identify which profile field needs updating.
2. Follow the organization's identity-verification process.
3. Check whether the customer has permission to update that field.
4. Use the approved profile-update procedure.
5. Confirm the result without exposing unnecessary personal information.

## 2. Service Availability

Problem: A customer asks whether a service is available.

Recommended steps:
1. Check the official service-status information.
2. Verify whether a known incident or scheduled maintenance affects the service.
3. Communicate only confirmed information.
4. If the status is unclear, escalate to the appropriate support team.

## 3. Submit a Support Request

Problem: A customer needs help with an issue that has not been resolved.

Recommended steps:
1. Record a clear subject and problem description.
2. Identify the affected service or feature.
3. Capture relevant timestamps and error details, if available.
4. Assign the ticket to the appropriate category.
5. Escalate urgent or unresolved issues according to support policy.

## 4. Customer Communication

Support responses should be polite, clear, and relevant to the customer's problem. Explain verified troubleshooting steps and ask only for information needed to investigate the issue.

Do not claim that an issue has been fixed unless the result has been verified.

## 5. When to Escalate

Escalate issues when they require specialist access, involve unresolved service failures, raise security or privacy concerns, or cannot be handled under the approved support procedures. Record the reason for escalation.
